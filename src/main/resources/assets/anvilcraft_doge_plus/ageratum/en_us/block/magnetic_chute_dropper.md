# Magnetic Chute Dropper

<block id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:magnetic_chute_dropper"/>

A hybrid of <ref item="anvilcraft:magnetic_chute"/> and a dropper: sucks items in from the side opposite the facing direction and drops them in the facing direction. Supports **upward** placement.

<recipe id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:crafting_shapeless/magnetic_chute_dropper"/>

::: info
All chute variants support item filtering, slot disabling, and comparator output — same as the base mod's chutes, and they can fire or drop up to nine times the amount.
:::
