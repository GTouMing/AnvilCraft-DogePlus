# Inlay Carrier

<block id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:inlay_carrier"/>

The Inlay Carrier is a **directional base material** of the inlay system: its 6 sockets each correspond to a face — inlay a logic gate material to turn that face into an input, an output or one of the gates, and transfer materials can be inlaid as well.

- Only faces that have been inlaid can connect to adjacent redstone dust/Redstone Wire.
- Used to craft the [Logic Carrier](logic_carrier.md) and the [Logistics Carrier](logistics_carrier.md).
