# Giant Doge Anvil

<block id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:giant_doge_anvil"/>

A 3×3×3 multiblock formed when a fully grown Doge Anvil transforms, which can also be obtained through the multiblock conversion recipe. It inherits the full Giant Anvil feature set, meaning it is another way to obtain the Giant Anvil, bypassing the corrupted beacon.
