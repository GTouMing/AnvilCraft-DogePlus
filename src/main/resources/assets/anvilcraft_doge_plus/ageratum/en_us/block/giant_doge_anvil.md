# Giant Doge Anvil

<block id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:giant_doge_anvil"/>

A giant anvil occupying a 3×3×3 space, used for processing that requires giant-anvil strikes.

## Use

- **Feeding and growth**: feed a [Doge Anvil](doge_anvil.md) raw meat until its growth cap. The small anvil is the bottom-center of the structure, and the other 26 cells must be replaceable.
- **Structure conversion**: 19 [Doge Steel Blocks](doge_steel_block.md) can also be arranged into the structure below for conversion.

See the [Doge Line](../mechanics/doge.md) for growth settings and retries.
