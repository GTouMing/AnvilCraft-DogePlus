# Doge Steel Block

<block id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:doge_steel_block"/>

The storage block for [Doge Steel Ingots](../item/doge_steel_ingot.md), used in the Doge Anvil recipe and the [Giant Doge Anvil](giant_doge_anvil.md) conversion structure.

- Craft 9 ingots into 1 block, or unpack 1 block into 9 ingots.
- An Iron Block and a Bone Block also produce a steel block through AnvilCraft's super-heating recipe.
- The giant structure uses steel blocks; feeding a small anvil is a separate route and does not require surrounding it with steel blocks.
