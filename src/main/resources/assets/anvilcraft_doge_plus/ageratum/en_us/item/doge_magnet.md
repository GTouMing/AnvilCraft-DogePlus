# Hand Doge Magnet

<item id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:doge_magnet"/>

A hand-held magnet that stores and launches anvils:

- **Right-click an anvil** — store it into the magnet.
- **Right-click a block** — place the stored anvil.
- **Right-click air (with an anvil stored)** — charge up, then **release** to launch, dealing damage to entities hit.
- **Right-click air (no anvil stored)** — attract nearby items and experience orbs.
- **Shift + right-click** — place a [Doge Node](../entity/doge_node.md).
- Repair it with Doge Steel Ingots.
