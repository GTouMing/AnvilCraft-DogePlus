# Doge Steel Ingot

<item id="anvilcraft_doge_plus:doge_steel_ingot"/>

The mod's basic material, superheated from an Iron Ingot and Bone Meal.

## Uses

- Craft a [Doge Anvil](../block/doge_anvil.md) or [Hand Doge Magnet](doge_magnet.md), and repair the latter.
- Craft 9 ingots into 1 [Doge Steel Block](../block/doge_steel_block.md), or unpack the block into 9 ingots.
- The four chute variants combine AnvilCraft chutes with vanilla dispensers or droppers; they do not require Doge Steel Ingots.
