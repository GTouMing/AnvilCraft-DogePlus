# AnvilCraft: Doge+ Handbook

Welcome to **AnvilCraft: Doge+**, an addon for [AnvilCraft](https://github.com/Anvil-Dev/AnvilCraft) built around the **Doge series** of materials.

This addon brings three pillars of content:

- **Doge Steel & Doge Tools** — a new material smelted from iron and bone, used to build the Doge Anvil, the Giant Doge Anvil, and a hand-held Doge Magnet.
- **The Inlay System** — a brand-new crafting mechanic: hammer inlay materials into base materials on the Inlay Table to grant them special properties.
- **Experience Polishing** — quality-of-life additions such as chute-dispenser hybrids, a more attractive Doge Node, and a wearable Mobile Silencer.
